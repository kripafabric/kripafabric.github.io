Artisan Foundation

Why Lucknow's Master Chikankari Artisans Trust Kripa Fabric

Authentic Chikankari embroidery involves over 32 distinct stitches—including delicate Tepchi, Bakhiya (shadow work), Phanda, and Jaali. Standard commercial fabrics often tear, warp, or pucker under the artisan’s needle.

At Kripa Fabric, our cloth materials are calibrated with precise yarn tensile strength, open square grid warp/weft balance, and residue-free finishing, making them effortless to puncture for jaali work while retaining shape after washing and dyeing.

Needle-Friendly Warp & Weft Count

Balanced weave count allows fine needles to glide through without snagging or leaving distorted puncture holes.

Post-Embroidery Dye Compatibility

Uniform absorption for pastel dyes (the hallmark of Chikankari) without streaking or differential cotton-thread fading.

Preshrunk & Decatized

Pre-conditioned to ensure that delicate shadow embroidery does not crimp or bunch up when washed.
